Privacy Policy

Publishing Poems · for the Facebook Page “তোমার নামে রোদ্দুর” · Last updated 13 October 2026

Publishing Poems is a private publishing tool run by the owner of the Facebook Page “তোমার নামে রোদ্দুর”. It turns the owner’s own Bangla poems into short videos and posts them to the owner’s own Facebook Page and YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

  • Page access: the name and ID of the Page the owner manages, and a Page access token so the app can post Reels to that Page.
  • Page and Reel statistics: totals such as plays, watch time, reactions, comments, shares and follower counts, so the owner can see how their Reels perform.

The app does not read private messages, does not collect names, profiles or contact details of people who view or react to the Page, and does not access any other Page or account.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner’s videos and to show the owner performance statistics.
  • Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner’s own computer. They are not uploaded to any other server.
  • Data is never sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Third-party services

The app talks only to Meta (Facebook Graph API) and Google (YouTube and Google Sheets, for the owner’s own content). Their own privacy policies apply to data held on their platforms.

Data deletion

To remove the app’s access, go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations and remove “Publishing Poems”. The app then deletes its stored token and statistics from the owner’s computer. You can also ask for deletion at the email address below; requests are handled within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
